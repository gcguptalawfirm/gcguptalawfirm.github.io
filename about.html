About Us

The legacy behind our name

GC Gupta Law Firm stands upon the proud legacy of Shri Ganga Charan Gupta, Retd. DSP — a man whose life continues to inspire us every single day. He is remembered for his unmatched dedication, integrity and commitment to public service.

Following his distinguished career in the police service, he brought the same principles of discipline, justice and duty into the legal field as an advocate at the Rajasthan High Court. His name represents not only excellence but also the values we uphold in every matter we take on.

At present, his son, Advocate Dr. Yogesh Kumar Gupta, upholds this legacy with over 35 years of legal practice across trial courts and High Courts throughout India. Under his leadership, the firm has grown into a respected name in the legal fraternity.
