Our Legacy

Built on a lifetime of service

Our firm stands upon the proud legacy of Shri Ganga Charan Gupta, Retd. DSP — honoured on Republic Day 1985 with a medal presented by Justice P.K. Banerjee, then Acting Governor of Rajasthan. After a distinguished career in the police service, he brought the same discipline, justice and duty to the Bar as an advocate at the Rajasthan High Court.

Today, his son Advocate Dr. Yogesh Kumar Gupta leads the firm, with over 35 years of legal practice across trial courts and High Courts throughout India.

  • Every matter — big or small — handled with the same dedication and sincerity
  • Regular appearances before trial courts, High Courts, the Supreme Court and tribunals
  • Practising in Jaipur, Jodhpur and Delhi, with matters across India
